About me

I went to Ulster University and got a degree in sports science - but uni never really felt like my thing, sitting in lectures didn't compare to actually coaching. Once I graduated I qualified as a PT and started taking clients in my home gym, and that's where I learned the most: working with people, seeing what works, and building from there. Now I help GAA players perform at their best.
